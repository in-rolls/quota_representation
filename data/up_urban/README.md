# UP urban heads, 2012–2017

This exploratory extension compares women's election in 2017 between offices reserved for women in 2012 and all other offices, restricting the outcome election to offices not reserved for women. Municipal council and town council heads receive equal office weights. Winner sex is recorded separately from reservation. No source election files are copied here: `scripts/13_up_urban.R` reads checksum-pinned releases from `local_elections_up` through `data/manifest.yaml`.

Links require the same office type, the same reviewed district, and exactly matching body names after Unicode NFC normalization and removal of whitespace and format characters. Vowels, consonants, diacritics, numerals and punctuation remain distinct. All records sharing an ambiguous identity are excluded. The crosswalk reconciles district spellings and the Allahabad/Prayagraj, Faizabad/Ayodhya and Sant Ravidas Nagar/Bhadohi names. Division membership follows the UP Planning Department's [Statistical Diary 2022, printed page 364](https://static.investindia.gov.in/s3fs-public/2023-04/Statistical%20Diary_Uttar%20Pradesh_2022.pdf#page=393). Exact names do not establish unchanged municipal boundaries.

The primary quantity is an unadjusted difference in female-winner probabilities. Secondary comparisons adjust for district, office type and prior caste reservation; separate the two office types; require identical source body spelling; and restrict to unchanged caste reservation. The last restriction conditions on a later assignment and changes the comparison. Intervals use division-clustered HC1 covariance with the cluster and regression degrees-of-freedom corrections and a t reference distribution with G−1 degrees of freedom. District clustering and leave-one-division-out results accompany the primary interval. A null-imposed Rademacher wild cluster bootstrap uses 9,999 draws across the 18 divisions, with the seed and inverted confidence interval saved in `tabs/up_urban_bootstrap.csv`. These choices were specified after inspecting schemas, geographic labels and marginal outcomes but before computing treatment contrasts; this is exploratory, not preregistered.

The upstream releases flag independent review and geographic validation as pending. Checking saved official HTML establishes fidelity to those published cells, not boundary continuity, contemporaneous accuracy, or random quota assignment. Section 9A of the [UP Municipalities Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/16228/1/municipalcorporation2016_eng.pdf) specifies population-based ordering and rotation. The exact historical implementation requires further verification; these comparisons do not enter the paper's causal summary or rural–urban meta-analysis.

`analysis.parquet` retains the linked source IDs and study recodes. Unmatched offices, coverage by prior quota, transition counts, estimates and influence results are generated separately. The 2012 source repeats Sidpura in Kasganj with different winners; both records are excluded from the one-to-one linkage.

Run `Rscript --vanilla scripts/13_up_urban.R` and then `Rscript --vanilla scripts/98h_validate_up_urban.R`. The validator independently reads all published district, body, reservation, winner-name and winner-sex cells from the saved official HTML in the sibling `local_elections_up/data/collections/sec_historical_results/` archives. Those archives are needed for the source audit as well as the manifest-pinned releases and parser provenance. Their content hashes must match the pinned provenance records. It also reconstructs clustered estimates independently and reproduces the seeded bootstrap interval.
