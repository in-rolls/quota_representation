\section{Women's Candidacy and Electoral Support}\label{sec:app_outcomes}
\suppressfloats[t]

Table~\ref{tab:candidacy_combined} reports candidacy and electoral-support outcomes for Rajasthan GPs open to women and men in 2020. The models include quota assignments in 2005, 2010, and 2015 and all their interactions; even-numbered columns add block fixed effects. The main-text comparison uses these columns to contrast reservation only in 2015 with no reservation in the three observed prior elections. The coefficient on the 2015 assignment identifies that contrast because the two earlier assignments are zero in both histories. Its estimates provide no clear evidence of increased candidacy or electoral support, but the intervals permit meaningful gains. Comparisons involving multiple reserved terms require sums of the relevant coefficients, rather than interpreting individual interaction coefficients as effects of those histories. The vote outcome includes only votes received by women among the winner and runner-up, divided by all valid votes.
